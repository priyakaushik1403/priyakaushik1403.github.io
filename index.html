Research

Understanding where safety mechanisms fall short—and how to evaluate them in the environments people actually use.

01 / Text moderation

Chat safety in Roblox

Analyzing harmful interactions that escape chat moderation and the strategies users employ to bypass filtering.

02 / Work in progress

Voice moderation in online gaming

Evaluating Roblox’s voice-safety classifier to identify harmful speech it misses and examine how speech characteristics and acoustic variations affect moderation decisions.

03 / Work in progress

Moderation in user-generated gaming environments

Investigating how inappropriate content becomes accessible through navigation and interactions in Roblox experiences, and whether moderation detects and restricts access to it.

04 / Co-author · Work in progress

Purpose of Life: A Utility-centric Taxonomy and Analysis of mHealth App Ecosystem

Contributing as a co-author to a utility-centric taxonomy and analysis of mHealth applications based on their intended purposes and functionalities.
